Fills

What supports the body

01

Memory foam

Pressure relief

Conforms to the body and returns slowly. Used where support matters more than a low profile — lumbar supports especially.

02

DuPont bio-fibre

Resilient loft

A synthetic down alternative. Holds loft and shape through washing, which is why it dominates the neck and lumbar ranges.

03

Silk-floss fill

Light and low-profile

Keeps a seat cushion thin so it does not raise the seating position. The default fill in the slim seat cushions.

04

High-rebound sponge

Firm, fast recovery

Returns immediately under load. Used where the cushion has to take repeated weight without packing down.
