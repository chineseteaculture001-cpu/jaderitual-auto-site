Why universal

The volume argument for one construction

A model-specific part carries tooling, a pattern library and a SKU count that multiplies with every facelift. A universal cushion or floor mat carries none of that — which is why those two are the ranges we lead with.

01

No tooling to amortise

Nothing is moulded to one vehicle's floor pan or seat base, so there is no development cost to recover inside the unit price. It is the single largest reason a universal line can be sold cheaply and still hold margin.

02

Stock depth instead of breadth

A handful of SKUs covers the range, so a distributor can hold real depth and ship same-day rather than carrying one piece of forty patterns.

03

Coverage that ages well

A new model year does not obsolete the item. The range keeps selling while the catalogue around it is rewritten.

04

Fitted, not fastened to a frame

A cushion fixes to the seat base and backrest independently and a mat holds on a non-slip backing, which is what lets one construction sit across saloons, SUVs and MPVs.

Custom & OEM

Built to your pattern when universal is not enough

Universal is the default, not the limit. Material, colour, stitching, logo, dimensions and packaging are all open to specification, and the pattern can be developed against your own seat drawings or a physical seat.

  • Dimensions and pattern — develop a specific seat base where your market needs one.
  • Material and fill — Nappa, PU, suede-touch, air-layer knit, flannel, TPE; memory foam, DuPont bio-fibre, high-rebound sponge.
  • Colour, quilting and stitching — including contrast stitch and perforation patterns.
  • Logo and private label — labels, tags, surface logo printing and branded packaging.
  • Pattern and mould — vehicle-pattern floor mats, including the mould itself, developed for the model you need.
  • Packing — retail cartons, barcodes and export cartons to your specification.
