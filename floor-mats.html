Lead range

Car floor matsuniversal-fit by the container, or moulded to the vehicle

Two ways to buy, and they suit different jobs. Universal-fit mats are cut to the common footwell range: no tooling, no per-model SKU count, and the version to buy in depth and reorder. Vehicle-pattern mats are moulded to one model's floor pan and are how a distributor covers a whole market without carrying a hundred part numbers. Either way the specification starts with the material.

Materials

Four materials, and two coverage grades

A mat is specified by its material and its coverage before anything else, because those two set the price and the market. Everything else — the vehicle, the colour, the logo — sits on top of that choice.

On "3D" and "5D" coverage

The range is offered in 3D and 5D coverage, but the industry has never settled what separates one grade from the next and every factory draws the line differently. So we quote the coverage you actually need against a physical sample rather than against a grade name — you see the edge height and the sill coverage before the order is placed.

One pattern per model, not one product per model

A vehicle-pattern programme is a development project, not a catalogue line: send the model or a set of the original mats and the mould is developed against it. That is what makes it workable for a distributor covering many models — the range grows by pattern, and the terms are agreed per project.

Vehicle coverage

Patterns already held

The factory behind this range holds 227 moulded mat products, 203 of them built to a specific vehicle. The marques it is asked for most include Toyota, Chevrolet, Kia, Tesla, Hyundai, Jeep, Ford, Honda, Mazda, Mitsubishi and others — and that list is not a limit. A pattern is developed from the original floor pan rather than adapted from a generic mat, so a model that is missing is a development job, not a dead end.

Orders for a vehicle pattern typically start at 10 sets, and mixed batches are accepted, which is what makes it workable to test a market across several models without committing to depth on any one of them. Double-layer construction and surface logo printing are available across most of the range.

Vehicle names appear here to say which floor pans a pattern has been developed for. Nothing on this site implies affiliation with, authorisation by, or endorsement from any vehicle manufacturer, and no manufacturer logo is used.
