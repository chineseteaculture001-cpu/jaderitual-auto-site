Why they sell

The cheapest way to lift an order

A neck-and-lumbar set is a low-unit-cost, low-freight, high-perceived-value line. It is the item a buyer adds once, sells through quickly and then reorders on its own — which is why it earns its own page rather than sitting in an accessories list.

  • Fits the same buyer as the seat cushion range.
  • Packs flat, so freight cost per unit stays low.
  • Colourways can match the seat range for a coherent set.
  • Available as a single, a pair or a matched two-piece set.
